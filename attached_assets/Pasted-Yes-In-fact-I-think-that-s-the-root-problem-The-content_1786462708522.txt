Yes. In fact, I think that's the root problem.

The content isn't weak.

The canvas orientation is wrong.

Right now you're trying to force an executive consulting story onto an 8.5 x 11 portrait page, which naturally creates:

Tall vertical layouts
Large white gaps
Tiny diagrams
Underutilized space

For ISL, I'd switch to:

Landscape Letter (11" × 8.5")

This immediately gives you room for a true consulting layout.

Example
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Logo  |  Headline                    │
├──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Problem   | BOS Framework | Outcomes │
├──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Before    | After         | Results  │
├──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Living 2 Live | RDF | CTA           │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘


Now every inch of space is working.
