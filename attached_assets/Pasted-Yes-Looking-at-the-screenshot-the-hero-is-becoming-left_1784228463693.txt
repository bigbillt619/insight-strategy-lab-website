Yes. Looking at the screenshot, the hero is becoming **left-heavy**.

The issue isn't that the graphic is too small—it's that the **headline has grown into 6 lines** while the BOS visualization remains roughly the same height. Your eye spends 80% of its attention on the left side.

### Current Visual Weight

**Left Column**

* Trust bar
* 4 credential badges
* Massive 6-line headline
* Supporting paragraph
* Emotional statement
* Framework statement
* 2 CTAs
* 4 benefit bullets

**Right Column**

* Single BOS diagram

The left side contains nearly twice the visual content.

***

## Option 1 (My Recommendation)

### Make the BOS Visualization 15-20% Larger

Increase the right column width from roughly:

```css
50% / 50%
```

to

```css
45% text
55% visualization
```

or

```css
grid-template-columns: 0.9fr 1.1fr;
```

The BOS framework is your differentiator. Give it more real estate.

***

## Option 2 (Best Conversion + Balance)

Shorten the headline slightly.

Current:

> Finally get your people, processes, technology, data, and AI working together in one Business Operating System.

Try:

> Finally get your people, processes, technology, data, and AI working together.

Then add directly below:

```text
Built on a proven Business Operating System framework.
```

or

```text
Using a Business Operating System designed for clarity, accountability, and growth.
```

Now the headline drops from 6 lines to 4-5 lines.

The visual instantly balances.

***

## Option 3 (My Favorite Layout)

Keep the headline.

Move this line:

```text
People + Processes + Technology + Data + AI = Business Operating System
```

into the BOS graphic.

For example:

Current BOS box:

```text
BUSINESS OPERATING SYSTEM

✓ Clarity

```

Change to:

```text
BUSINESS OPERATING SYSTEM

People + Processes +
```

## What I Would Do

If this were my site, I'd make three changes:

1. Increase BOS visualization size by \~20%.
2. Move the "People + Processes + Technology + Data + AI = BOS" statement into the graphic.
3. Collapse the four credentials into one trust row.

That would create nearly perfect visual balance:

```text
LEFT                        RIGHT

Headline                    BOS Framework

Subheadline                 Framework Flow

Emotional Statement         Outcome Box

CTA                         Transformation Visual

Benefits
```
