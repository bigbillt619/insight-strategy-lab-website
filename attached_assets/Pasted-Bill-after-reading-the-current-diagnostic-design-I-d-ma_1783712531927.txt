Bill, after reading the current diagnostic design, I'd make a more fundamental change.

Right now the tool is essentially:

```text
5 Questions
      ↓
Recommendation
      ↓
Lead Capture
```

That's useful, but it positions ISL as a company that recommends tools.

Your vision is much bigger.

You're not selling software recommendations.

You're selling **Business Operating System Architecture.**

***

# The Problem With The Current Diagnostic

Current questions:

* Business Type
* Company Size
* Biggest Bottleneck
* Current Tools
* Revenue Range

These are mostly demographic qualifiers.

At the end, the system says:

> "Based on your answers, we recommend..."

which feels similar to dozens of AI and software assessment tools.

It doesn't showcase the ISL methodology.

***

# Reframe Around the ISL Operating System

Instead of diagnosing:

```text
What software should you use?
```

Diagnose:

```text
How mature is your Business Operating System?
```

This instantly differentiates ISL.

***

# Recommendation Engine Upgrade

I'd also move away from the current flat-rule approach.

Current:

```text
Question
    ↓
Answer
    ↓
Recommendation
```

Future:

Each answer receives a weighted score:

```text
Strategy
People
Processes
Technology
Data
AI
```

Then calculate:

```text
BOS Maturity Score
```

This removes hundreds of brittle recommendation mappings and makes the assessment scalable.
